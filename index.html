On the road

About

12 years of telling stories that matter

I started in 2013 at a digital marketing agency, launching campaigns for Philips, CaratLane and Bigbasket. It taught me what makes people click, watch and share.

A master’s in New Media Storytelling at USC Annenberg took me to Los Angeles. There I produced immersive journalism for NPR, Al Jazeera, KCRW and Google, worked on advocacy campaigns at 72andSunny, and made a VR documentary on deportation that won a Webby Award.

Since 2019 I’ve brought that mix to the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), first in New Delhi and now on its global channels in New York. I plan, write, shoot and edit campaigns on climate change, gender equality, poverty, crisis and digital transformation for audiences worldwide.

I work where strategy meets creativity. I write the brief, shape the idea, plan the distribution, make the film, and read the data that shows what worked.
