HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE
========================

Your site is 4 files plus 2 folders. You only ever touch 3 things:

1. COLOURS AND FONTS  ->  style.css  (the first 20 lines, marked "EDIT COLOURS AND FONTS HERE")
   Change a hex code, e.g. --accent: #0d6b62;  ->  --accent: #b03a2e;
   Find colour codes at https://htmlcolorcodes.com

2. VIDEOS, PHOTOS, LINKS  ->  content.js
   - Latest work slider (top of the page): 5 or more videos
   - Video wall slider (Reels): 6 or more videos
   - Field photo slider (About section)
   To add a video: copy one { ... }, block, paste it, change the words.
   For YouTube, paste only the ID: in https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AbC123xyz the ID is AbC123xyz

3. TEXT  ->  index.html
   Every sentence on the page is in this file. Search for the words (Ctrl/Cmd + F) and type over them.
   Only change the words between the < > tags, not the tags themselves.

Photos go in /images, video files in /videos (each under 25 MB).
Keep photos under ~400 KB each: compress them free at https://squoosh.app

Don't edit script.js.

After you save a change on GitHub, the live site updates in about a minute.
