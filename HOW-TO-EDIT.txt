HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE
========================

EASY WAY (no code): app.pagescms.org
1. Go to app.pagescms.org and sign in with GitHub.
2. Open "rajavenkatapathy", then "Website content".
3. Change any text, add videos to "Latest work" or "Video wall", upload photos.
4. Click Save. The live site updates in about a minute.

Adding a video: in "Latest work", click "Add an entry", fill in the title, date and role,
and paste the YouTube link. For YouTube you don't need a cover image.
Newest first: drag the new entry to the top of the list.

COLOURS: open style.css on github.com, click the pencil, change the colour codes in
the first 20 lines, and commit. Colour codes: https://htmlcolorcodes.com

Text that isn't in the editor (work experience, awards, toolkit) is in index.html on github.com.
